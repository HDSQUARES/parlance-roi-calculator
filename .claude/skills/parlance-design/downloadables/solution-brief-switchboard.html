The challenge

Hospital switchboards field huge volumes of calls — wrong numbers, requests that should be self-service, and operators stuck searching directories to route callers. Routine work crowds out the calls that genuinely need a person.

How Parlance helps

  • Proprietary name recognition understands physician names, departments, and clinical terms from natural speech.
  • Real-time routing connects callers to the right team the first time, drawing on live HL7 directory data.
  • Engineered safety escalates to a human operator instantly whenever a call needs one.
  • Always-on coverage answers as many calls as volume demands, day or night.
