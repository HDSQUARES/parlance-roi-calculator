Case Study

How Virtua Health doubled call volume — without adding staff

200K → 400K
Annual calls handled, same team size

The challenge

As Virtua Health grew, inbound patient call volume climbed sharply. Adding contact-center staff to keep pace wasn't sustainable — and longer waits risked the consistent patient experience the system is known for.

The solution

Virtua deployed Parlance Voice AI across patient access. Conversational AI now answers routine calls, recognizes physician names, and routes callers to the right team automatically — escalating to staff only when a human is needed.

The results

  • Annual call volume doubled to 400,000 with no added headcount
  • Consistent patient experience maintained at scale
  • Operators freed to focus on complex, high-value conversations
