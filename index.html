How we calculate this
Reduce staffing costsCalls × 25% deflection × 4-min handle time × 20% cash realization × loaded rate. Only the freed time that avoids overtime or a new hire is counted. (MGMA DataDive 2025)
Reduce turnoverOperators × 30% annual turnover × 10% Parlance reduction × $12,000 cost per hire. (SHRM 2025)
Streamline call managementMisrouted-call time (15% × 85 sec) + after-call documentation (30% of calls × 2 min × 30% automated), held to a conservative floor. QA/supervisor time is excluded — that capacity is still needed. (MGMA 2024, Hyro.ai, CloudTalk 2026)
Increase FCRCalls × 15% repeat-call rate × 10% improvement × 4-min handle time × loaded rate. (DialogHealth 2025)
Reclaim revenue(Calls × $0.32 client-validated per-call ceiling) − operational savings. The ceiling is the blend of Allina Health ($0.278/call) and Virtua Health ($0.369/call), so the tool can never claim more than real clients demonstrate.
The revenue line captures losses from: 7% call abandonment in healthcare contact centers (DialogHealth 2025), ~30% of inbound calls being new-patient inquiries (MGMA 2025), 27% of abandoned callers going to a competitor (CCD Care 2025), a $300–500 new-patient appointment value (HFMA 2025), and a $3,150 primary-care patient lifetime value (Levo Health 2025). The $0.32 ceiling updates as Parlance documents more clients.
